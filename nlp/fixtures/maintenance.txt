Title: Crowdsourced School Infrastructure Maintenance
Abstract
School infrastructure maintenance often relies on paper reports and delayed inspections.
This project develops a crowdsourcing platform for damage reporting and maintenance prioritization.
Mobile applications allow community members to submit geotagged photographs of damaged facilities.
A maintenance dashboard displays repair requests, location maps, and repair status.
Statement of the Problem
Fragmented damage reporting prevents timely maintenance prioritization.
Facility managers lack reliable location data and transparent repair tracking.
Objectives
Develop a crowdsourcing platform for school infrastructure maintenance.
Identify and categorize recurring maintenance issues. Design and implement geospatial visualization.
Compare response times before and after deployment. Assess willingness to establish transparent repair tracking.
Methodology
Geospatial visualization groups geotagged reports by location.
Duplicate report detection reduces redundant repair requests.
Priority scheduling considers damage severity and safety risks.
Background
Community participation can improve public facility management and accountability.
Scope
The pilot covers classrooms, drainage facilities, and electrical fixtures.
