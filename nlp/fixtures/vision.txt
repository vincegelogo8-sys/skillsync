Title: Deep Learning for Plant Disease Image Classification
Abstract
This study investigates computer vision for automated plant disease detection.
Convolutional neural networks classify leaf images into healthy and diseased categories.
Transfer learning adapts pretrained image classification models to a small agricultural dataset.
Image processing removes background noise and normalizes illumination.
Objectives
Develop plant disease detection using convolutional neural networks.
Evaluate image classification under variable lighting conditions.
Compare transfer learning with training from scratch.
Methodology
Data augmentation applies rotations and color transformations to leaf images.
Stratified cross-validation estimates classification accuracy on unseen samples.
Confusion matrices reveal false positives and confusion between disease categories.
Background
Manual crop inspection is slow and requires agricultural expertise.
Computer vision can support early diagnosis and reduce crop losses.
Scope
The prototype processes tomato leaf images using a mobile application.
